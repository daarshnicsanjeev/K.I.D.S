Step 2: Connect to Gemini Spark

Your school assistant server is active! Follow the steps below to connect your server to Gemini Spark.

🔗 Open Connected Google Apps Script Project

⚙️ Step-by-Step Connection Instructions:

  1. Click Copy Link above to copy your personal server URL.
  2. Click the button below to open Gemini Spark Apps.
  3. In Gemini Spark, go to Connected Apps.
  4. Click Add a custom app.
  5. Paste your copied server link into the Add a custom app link field.
  6. Click Next, then click Authorize to finish!
✨ Open Gemini Spark Apps

✨ Open Gemini Spark Skills

  2. Click Open Gemini Spark Skills and upload the SKILL.md file.
